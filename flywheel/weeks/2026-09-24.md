# Flywheel Why — 2026-09-24

Picks: SO, DUK, AVGO, DECK, MS

## Macro backdrop
Fed HIKED on Sep 16, 2026 (first hike since 2023), to 3.75-4.00%.
Energy-driven inflation. Dot plot: 8 members see 2 more hikes by end of 2027.
2Y 4.74%, 20Y 5.39%. Every sector red on the day.
Rate-sensitive stocks (utilities especially) got hit — that's likely
why the technicals flagged SO and DUK as oversold.

## Same-bet warning
**SO + DUK = one bet** (2 of 5 picks). Both are regulated utilities at
52-week lows because of the rate hike. If rates keep rising they fall
together. If the Fed signals "one and done" they bounce together.

## SO — Southern Company — $83.34
- **What:** Southeast US electric/gas utility (Georgia Power, Alabama Power). Dividend aristocrat.
- **Why up:** Sitting right on its 52-week low ($83.17 vs $100.84 high).
  ~3.6% dividend yield. Real data-center story: Google nuclear-upgrade deal
  (Sep 21, +96 MW), Robins battery storage online. Earnings growth driven by data-center load.
- **Why not:** Rates. If more hikes come, bond yields at 5%+ make a 3.6% utility
  yield less attractive. High payout ratio, modest revenue growth.
- **Main reason to grade:** Rate fears ease / stock bounces off 52-wk low.

## DUK — Duke Energy — $114.42
- **What:** Carolinas/Florida/Midwest electric utility.
- **Why up:** At 52-week low ($113.90 low, $134.49 high). EPS growth target 6.5%+,
  capex rising under the Carolinas Resource Plan (bigger rate base = more earnings).
  Data-center demand in the Carolinas. Earnings due ~early November.
- **Why not:** Same rate problem as SO. Morgan Stanley cut target $133 -> $127 (Sep 18).
  Consensus is only Hold. Storm-season costs.
- **Main reason to grade:** Rate fears ease / bounce off 52-wk low.

## AVGO — Broadcom — $354.99
- **What:** Chipmaker + infrastructure software. Designs custom AI chips for Google, Meta, OpenAI.
- **Why up:** Down ~28% from $495 high while the business is booming: Q3 (Sep 2)
  AI chip revenue $16.7B, +221% YoY, EPS beat ($3.32 vs $3.16). Management reaffirmed
  $115B AI revenue FY27 and $230B FY28. Avg analyst target ~$532 (+50%).
  Classic "great business, stock lagging" setup.
- **Why not:** Q4 revenue guide slightly light ($34.8B vs $35.0B). Priced on huge
  future numbers — any AI capex slowdown talk hits it hard. Rising rates hurt
  high-multiple growth stocks.
- **Main reason to grade:** Stock re-rates toward targets on AI demand.

## DECK — Deckers (HOKA, UGG) — ~$80.23
- **What:** Footwear — HOKA running shoes, UGG boots.
- **Why up:** Cheap: ~11x earnings, down 17% in ~4 months. Management raised full-year
  EPS and margin guidance. Margins still strong (gross 53%+). UGG season (Q4 holiday)
  is its biggest quarter. Next earnings ~late October.
- **Why not:** Growth is decelerating — HOKA hit single-digit growth (+7.7%) for the
  first time, UGG +4.9%. Market treats it as a former growth stock; cheap can stay cheap.
- **Main reason to grade:** Late-Oct earnings shows HOKA stabilizing.

## MS — Morgan Stanley — $196.26
- **What:** Investment bank + big wealth management business.
- **Why up:** Earnings Oct 14 with analysts expecting double-digit revenue/EPS growth.
  Higher rates can help net interest income; deal and trading activity strong.
- **Why not:** Leaked client deal list in Asia — regulators in India and China
  looking at it. Reputational/fine risk. Banks sell off if the hike slows the economy.
- **Main reason to grade:** Oct 14 earnings beat, leak fades.

## Why it might go up (the reasoning)

**SO + DUK (utilities):** Utilities trade like bonds — people own them for the dividend.
When the Fed hiked, safe bonds suddenly paid more, so money left utilities for bonds.
That selloff was about rates, not the companies, and it pushed both to 52-week lows.
Meanwhile the businesses are improving: AI data centers need huge amounts of power and
these companies sell it. If the market decides this was "one hike and done," the rate fear
lifts and money flows back into good businesses that got sold for no company-specific reason.

**AVGO:** The business is racing ahead while the stock went backwards, and that gap usually
closes. AI chip sales for Google/Meta/OpenAI just tripled, but the stock is ~28% off its high
on fear the AI spending boom slows. Every quarter the big spenders keep spending, that fear
shrinks and the stock tends to catch up to the earnings.

**DECK:** The market punished slowing growth and overdid it. It went from hot growth stock to
~11x earnings (value-stock pricing) while still growing and very profitable. Holiday quarter
(UGG season) is its biggest. If late-Oct earnings show HOKA's slowdown has stopped getting worse,
it re-prices up — "slowing growth" priced like "no growth" is a mispricing.

**MS:** Higher rates can help banks earn more on money they lend/hold. Earnings Oct 14 — a strong
report on deals and trading plus the Asia leak story fading could lift it. Banks often rally
into and after solid earnings.

**Common thread:** 4 of 5 were sold for reasons outside the business (the rate hike, sector fear)
while the businesses stayed healthy. That's what an oversold technical signal often catches —
and they tend to bounce once the fear eases.
